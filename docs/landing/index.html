About the prompt

What was asked for, and what came back.

The brief asked for a complete, premium, and highly interactive portfolio website for a 3D artist — dark, sleek, minimalist, immersive, with a hero, a portfolio gallery, an about section and a contact form.

All three models returned a product showcase for a fictional audio brand instead. None shipped a gallery, an about section or a contact form. The divergence being unanimous points at the prompt rather than at any one model: premium, immersive, 3D, dark, Awwwards-winning is a far louder signal than portfolio for a 3D artist, and the strongest examples of that aesthetic are product launch pages.

Because that divergence is identical across all three, it separates none of them. The ranking scores what does: the mandated stack, the immersive aesthetic, the interactive 3D hero, hover and transition craft, responsiveness, accessibility and performance.
